PRINCIPLES

ANCHOR, ORDER, PRICE — IN THAT ORDER

We rebuilt Harlow Bay Studio's deck around three rules: open on the single strongest image, sequence the work by the outcome a client recognizes, and never show a rate without the proof standing next to it.

  • Hero spread first — no logo page, no team photo
  • Group work by client goal, not by shoot date
  • Every rate tier sits beside the deliverable that earns it
