The Governing Thought 02
Why Now

250 bps of margin is sitting in three fixable drivers.

Meridian's EBITDA margin sits at 4.1%, 250 bps below the 6.6% peer median for regional grocers of similar scale. Six weeks of store visits, P&L teardown, and vendor file review found the gap concentrated in three places.

  • Shrink runs 2.1% of COGS against a 1.3% peer benchmark, worst in perishables
  • Overtime hours run 18% over plan, driven by manual store-by-store scheduling
  • Vendor rebates are captured at 71%, 21 points below the contracted rate

The Options, Ranked 06
Five Moves

Five moves, ranked by impact and speed.

Every option on the table was scored on margin impact and time to cash; these five clear the bar.

  • Perishables shrink program — new markdown cadence and waste tracking in the 20 highest-shrink stores. 60 bps, live in 8 weeks.
  • Centralized scheduling engine — replaces manual store scheduling with a demand-based tool. 70 bps, live in 12 weeks.
  • Vendor rebate recovery — audit and rebill 18 months of unbilled deductions. 45 bps, cash in 6 weeks.
  • Assortment rationalization — cut the bottom 8% of SKUs by velocity. 40 bps, live in 16 weeks.
  • Top-12 vendor renegotiation — align terms to actual volume tiers. 35 bps, live in 20 weeks.
